Three.js loading notes
======================
These GLBs already contain the beige PBR material.

Recommended:
renderer.outputColorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace;
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.toneMappingExposure = 1.0;

Do NOT override the sofa materials with a white MeshStandardMaterial after GLTFLoader loads them.

Animation node names:
- drake-2-seater-console-v3.glb: console_lid
- drake-recliner-v3.glb: recliner_back, footrest

Orientation:
- metres
- Y up
- underside Y=0
- X/Z centered
- seat faces -Z
- backrest +Z
